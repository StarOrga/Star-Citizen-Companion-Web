## New regressions

- Phone ship detail, scrolled: the floating FAB column covers part of the Hangar button in the Haben-card at one scroll position (ph-ship-scrolled.png). Minor and transient, but the same class as Q4, which only had the first viewport checked.
- The large-popup open frame cost was not reproduced as improved (7 and 4 frames over 33 ms), see the table.

No console, network or horizontal-overflow regressions.

## Not verified

Projector newest-first order, Galerie planet banding, the E2 title-scan and stand beats by eye (numeric search start only), the 420 ms reduced-motion fade measured in the browser (read from source), 60 and 144 Hz behaviour (cannot be forced headless), a third popup run, a quiet-machine repeat of the scroll sweep.

## Overall verdict

The timing and behaviour fixes hold: E2 search now starts at 3.52 s on the qa-report-2 reference, E4 title and search beats are inside the claimed window, prefetch loads route code and data on hover with no errors, reduced motion uses no View Transition, lets clicks through and does not double content, the three small menus close as a staged reverse, and the ship entrance never goes dark on desktop or phone. Not met: the frame-cost claims. Cold HQ to Verse improved in the median (desktop 72 ms, phone 117 ms) but still has about 5 frames over 33 ms; the large-popup open showed 4 to 7 frames over 33 ms against the claimed 2; the scroll sweep is no better than qa-report-2. One small new phone finding (FAB column over the Hangar button when scrolled). Acceptable apart from the frame budget, which the user should decide on, and the two unverified visual items.
