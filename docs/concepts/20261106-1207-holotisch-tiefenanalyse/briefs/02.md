### The three designs (what differs)

They differ only in frame weight, tab switching, strip and Einsatz signal (P5, P6, P2, P8). Three real directions, all noticeably lighter than round 1; no double frames anywhere.

**A „Glas“**: no frames at all. The model lies directly on the starfield with its glow; the right panel is one single sheet of glass with one hairline. Tabs: a glowing pill that slides between icon + word on a 180 ms ease, in the skin of the fusion header. Strip: one capsule in the header's skin, values large, separators fade out. Einsatz signal: the whole page's light takes on the Einsatz hue very faintly (a large soft glow behind model and panel), plus symbol at the panel title and at the strip.

**B „Linie“**: one hairline splits model and panel, nothing else. Tabs: words with an animated underline and a short light sweep along the line on change, count badge per tab. Strip: four equal segments, each with its label, its lead value and one thin bar relative to the group median, no P-number. Einsatz signal: only lines and symbol change colour (strip edge, tab underline, dot rings, hexagon line).

**C „Instrument“**: the panel has a vertical icon rail on its left edge like a cockpit mode selector, active mode with a lit indicator that slides; panel and rail share one glass body. Strip: cockpit dock with small ring gauges (no number in the ring), and the Einsatz as 7 inline symbols directly in the strip instead of a popup: one click changes it, the active one carries its name. Einsatz signal: symbol and colour on hexagon fill, dots and active strip symbol.

Hue rule: Alles = the app's accent `#52c1e6` („Alles gleich scc blau ist fein“). Others get their own quiet hue plus a symbol. Project rules forbid red (`--sc-accent-hot`, elevated access; `--sc-danger`, errors) and `#f0c27b` (`--sc-warning`, worse comparison) for this purpose, so do not use those. Schleichen: a muted violet, for example `#9d8cff`. Say in the view that a colour never carries the meaning alone: the name and symbol stay.

Overview attributes per design: `data-design-motto`, `data-design-traits` (3, separated by `|`), `data-design-icon`, as in round 1. `data-design-active="true"` on the first, `hidden` on the others.

**d2-words „Vergleichsgruppe, Wörter“.** The owner flagged this one (long hesitation), so ask anew with a concrete example, never repeat it verbatim. Answer the owner's question first in `data-situation`: Ja, „Karriere“ und „Rolle“ sind RSIs eigene Begriffe: Karriere ist die breite Gruppe (Kampf), Rolle die engere darin (Leichter Jäger) (Comm-Link „The Shipyard: Careers and Roles“, in den Spieldaten `vehicleCareer` und `vehicleRole`). Einsatz ist unser eigenes Wort für das, was du unten wählst. Die Verwirrung kommt daher, dass „Kampf“ beides sein kann: Karriere des Schiffs und gewählter Einsatz. Question: Wie heißt die Auswahl in der Einordnung? Options: `names` (rec) „Alle Schiffe · Alle Kampfschiffe · Alle Leichten Jäger“: nur die Gruppe selbst, kein Fachwort, Rolle bleibt Standard. `terms` „Alle Schiffe · Karriere: Kampf · Rolle: Leichter Jäger“: RSIs Wörter sichtbar plus Wert. `today` „Alle Schiffe · Karriere · Rolle“: wie jetzt, der Wert fehlt. Viz: bars 350 / Kampfschiffe (unknown, do not give a number, use only Alle Schiffe 350 and Leichte Jäger 26).

**d2-effect „Was der Einsatz am Modell ändert“.** Situation: you wrote that another Einsatz shows other points in the 3D model. Measured today: 34 points under every Einsatz. Also: Alles, Kampf and Reisen show the same hexagon, only Schleichen changes it. Question: Was passiert am Modell beim Wechsel des Einsatzes? Options: `lift` (rec) relevant parts stay lit, others go quiet but stay reachable; `only` only relevant parts have points; `none` model stays as it is, only the Einordnung changes. Note the catch: which parts count as relevant must come from existing data, not invented (Kampf: Waffen, Raketen, Schilde. Schleichen: Kühler, Schilde, Gegenmaßnahmen, as a proposal).

**d2-alles „Was Alles bedeutet“.** Situation: Alles is internally the Kampf profile, so Alles and Kampf are identical (real values above). „Alles“ then promises something it does not do. **d2-room „Platz für Callouts“.** Situation: callouts never over the model means the model only gets the middle. Measured geometry above. Options: `reserve` (rec) gutters always free, nothing moves; large parts of the stage stay empty until something is selected, the model stays a little smaller, nothing moves under the mouse; `glide` model fills the stage and only slides aside on click; hover callouts then have no space and move the targets.
