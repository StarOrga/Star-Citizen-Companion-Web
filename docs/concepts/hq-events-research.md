## 3. Taxonomy

The types are not fixed. Treat each event as a record along these dimensions:

- **Venue:** PU, Arena Commander, Star Marine, website, Twitch Drops, or off-platform (Bar Citizen, contests).
- **Location binding:** none, system, specific location, or any of a set.
- **Progress scope:** personal, party-shared (Discovery Month has this), org, or global community.
- **Reward channel:** pledge/hangar, in-game, both, or cosmetic.
- **Delivery timing:** immediate, daily, after event end, or at a later attribution date.
- **Repeatability and caps:** once-only contracts, daily limits.
- **Prerequisites:** patch version, package ownership, a specific pledge on the account, an RSI account only.
- **Termination:** fixed date, patch-bound, or open-ended.

Observed or inferred types:

- **Free Fly and ship showcase** (IAE, Invictus/DefenseCon, Foundation Festival). Dates are fixed, rewards are access and sales, and the PU is open to everyone. This is not a reward-farming event.
- **PU contract events with reward tracks** (Discovery Month). Personal points, in-game permanent rewards, tied to a patch, and the end date can be vague.
- **AC leaderboard or participation** (Day of the Vara Kill Collector). The reward is delivered after the event.
- **Website advent or login rewards** (Luminalia: unlock one gift per day at 16:00 UTC, added to the hangar, with only an RSI account needed). Sources: https://www.gaminerd.com/star-citizens-luminalia-2955-event-brings-13-days-of-gifts-and-festivities/ and https://gg.deals/freebie/get-free-star-citizen-luminalia-goodies-daily-during-the-seasonal-in-game-festival/.
- **Seasonal PU gift events** (Luminalia gift boxes and delivery missions, 500 aUEC per box per https://starcitizen.tools/Luminalia).
- **Patch-linked limited-time PU events** (Clearing The Air).
- **Contests** (pumpkin, greeting cards) that award ships or items.
- **Twitch Drops**, as in the Phoenix Insignia item.
- *[memory, unverified]* Dynamic community-phase PU events (XenoThreat, Nine Tails). Community-goal events (Save Stanton). Referral and subscriber events.

Typical durations seen here: Free Fly 12–15 days, Day of the Vara about 19 days, Luminalia 13 days.

## 4. Where event information is published

- **Comm-links:** `/en/comm-link/transmission/<id>-<Slug>` (ids 21334, 21337, 21350, 21422, 21431 seen). Patch notes use `/en/comm-link/Patch-Notes/<id>-<Slug>`. Index: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link.
- **Event landing pages:** `/en/invictuslaunchweek2953`, `/en/invictuslaunchweek2954`.
- **Spectrum:** `/spectrum/community/SC/forum/1/thread/<slug>` (Announcements). Hotfix Central threads are in forum 190048.
- **Weekly posts:** "This Week in Star Citizen" and the "Roadmap Roundup", published every two weeks.
- **Structured data:** the official comm-link has an Add-to-calendar file with exact start and end times (reported by freeflyevent.com). The community wiki has "Quick facts" infoboxes with start date and system, and a "Narrative events" category.
- **Not found / unverified:**
  - I did not find an official JSON API or GraphQL endpoint for events.
  - *[memory]* An official comm-link RSS feed may exist.
  - *[memory]* The community Star Citizen Wiki API has no confirmed events endpoint.
- **Obstacles:**
  - My fetch of the RSI comm-link and Spectrum pages returned only meta tags, so the body appears client-rendered or restricted.
  - The Spectrum pages carry a CSRF token in their meta tags.
  - I did not test CORS. Plan on a server-side fetch with caching.
  - Third-party sites repeat dates and sometimes copy last year's. The IAE 2955 dates were reused as guesses for 2956.

## 5. How events end

- **Fixed dates:** Day of the Vara (9 Nov), IAE (27 Nov), the Free Fly events. Luminalia's calendar ends on its last day.
- **Patch-launched with a vague end:** Discovery Month began with 4.10.2 and its end is disputed. Clearing The Air began with 4.6.
- **Cadence:** CIG moved to bi-monthly major patches in 2026, with interstitial "content chunks" that can include in-game events (https://massivelyop.com/2026/01/23/star-citizen-confirms-a-switch-to-bi-monthly-major-patches-and-interstitial-content-chunks/). The first mid-patch Content Update was on 2026-02-11 (https://starcitizen.tools/Update:Star_Citizen_Alpha_4.6.0). Events can therefore start and end without a patch.
- **Rewards after the end:** the Day of the Vara mask is delivered after the event. The Mk IV paint is attributed again a few days after Discovery Month closes.
- **Outliving the event:** leftover IAE guards and NPCs were still in Orison after IAE, per the 4.5.0 notes.
- **Roadmap:** the Nov 4 Roadmap Roundup renames Alpha 4.11 to Alpha 5.0 (https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21422-Roadmap-Roundup-November-4-2026).

**Modelling consequence:** store `end` as nullable with an `end_source` and `end_confidence` field. Keep a separate `reward_delivery` object with a channel and a timing rule. Keep the program-level end separate from the reward-attribution date.

# Code sweep (branch claude/nachschub-hq-concept-9ffa89, 2026-11-08)

## Summary

Nachschub → Einsatz → step is modelled in the DB and in pure TS. A step's place is optional at every layer, and a supply line closes on inventory rise or a manual tick. Nothing in the code represents an Event acquisition path. The only "event" code is the `sc_events` timeline and game-live events. The official patch date is already fetched and displayed, but the next-patch estimate ignores it.

## 1. Nachschub / Einsatz model

- **Nachschub** (a package): `Nachschub` with `status` active|archived, `slot` 1..7 (`NACHSCHUB_MAX_SLOTS = 7`), and `snapshot` (inventory per supply-line id at planning time), `nachschub.model.ts:12`, `:18`, `:21-31`. Table `hq_supply_packages` (per the doc comment at `:20`).
- **Supply line / wish**: `hq_supplies` (`20261101000000_hq_supplies.sql:113`). Constraints: `qty 1..999` (`:134`), `class_name` ≤200 and nullable (`:136`), `source in (manual, set, search, live)` (`:140`).
- **Einsatz**: `hq_einsaetze` (`20261101010000_hq_einsaetze.sql:13`). `start_location text` nullable (`:20`, length ≤200 at `:33`). `status in (planned, active, done, archived)` (`:32`). `activity` is nullable and checked at `:30`.
- **Steps**: `hq_einsatz_steps` (`:54`). Columns: `kind` (not null, `:58`), `label`, `location` nullable (`:60`), `system` nullable (`:61`), `class_name` (`:62`), `qty`, `mission_ref` (`:65`), `state` todo|done (`:66`, `:85`), `done_by` user|live (`:86`). `kind` check is at `:75`. Max 40 steps per Einsatz (`:115`), `ord` 0..99 (`:74`).
- **Action types**: `StepKind` is `(typeof STEP_KINDS)[number]` at `src/app/shared/step/sc-step.component.ts:6`. The seven are buy, mission, mine, refine, craft, transport, pickup (`src/app/hq/nachschub/step-icons.ts:9-15`). The 7 icons are `STEP_ICON_PATHS` at `step-icons.ts:8-16`, plus `TRANSPORT_WHEELS` (`:19-22`) and the Nachschub tray icon (`:25`).
- **Proposal roles**: only four Einsatz roles exist: `mission | buy | mine | craft` (`src/app/hq/plan/einsatz-proposal.ts:18-19`). `refine`, `transport` and `pickup` are step kinds but never proposed roles. Craft is always last (`einsatz-proposal.ts:12`).
- **Snapshot / supply-line close**: `LineState` and `lineStatus` (`nachschub.model.ts:161-194`). A line closes only on a tick (`:191`) or an inventory rise ≥ need against the snapshot baseline (`:192`). It never closes by time (comment `:182-185`). `withBaseline` stores a baseline for lines that lack one (`:197-199`). `evaluateNachschub` is at `:334`.
- **Hangar origin**: `hangar_ships.origin` with values `pledge | ingame | loan` (Leihschiff), null = unknown (`supabase/migrations/20261107190000_wishlist_qty.sql:7`, `:53`, `:56`). I did not find a matching TS type in `src/app/hq`, so the TS side is unverified.

## 2. Place and route graphic

- **Place type**: `MapPlace { id, system, planet: string | null, location: string }` (`src/app/hq/plan/map/hq-map.types.ts:9-15`). Planet nullable means "open space" (`src/app/hq/plan/map/hq-route.ts:62-63`). Location is free text in the DB (`hq_einsatz_steps.location`, `hq_einsaetze.sql:60`, ≤200 chars).
- **Step with no place**: allowed. `hq_einsatz_steps.location` is nullable (`hq_einsaetze.sql:60`). The proposal's `DraftStep.location` is `string | null` (`einsatz-proposal.ts:44`).
- **Wie route (`buildRoute`)**: steps with no place are dropped. `if (!n.place || n.status === 'done') continue;` (`hq-route.ts:102`), with the header comment at `:9`.
- **Route graphic (`layoutRoute`)**: a step with no location becomes an "open" node labelled `openLabel` (`route-layout.ts:75-76`, `:123`). A step with no system inherits the previous step's system, else the first named system, else `unknownSystem` (`route-layout.ts:88-96`). So "system X, any place" can render in the graphic, but not in `buildRoute` or the proposal (`ProposalCtx.placeOf` returns `MapPlace | null`, `einsatz-proposal.ts:105`).
- **Non-PU venue**: no type or flag. Free text in `location` is the only option. There is no venue or event-site entity.
- **Not found**: an explicit "any place in system X" selector, and a venue type.
