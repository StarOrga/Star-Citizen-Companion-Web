## What round 2 must show

The head now has to carry FOUR things, compactly, for ships AND items: **what it is** (ship or item: name, manufacturer · class), **which patch** (short form only, e.g. `4.10.2-LIVE ▾`, NO build number / "Daten … · Schema" line anywhere), **owned yes/no** (Hangar for ships, Inventar for items), **which variant** (only when the ship is in the Hangar; it switches the whole page, so it belongs in the head). The rest stays in d3's panel card ("Dein Exemplar"/"Haben?"): wishlist with quantity stepper, rename, note, pin, remove (danger), "Im HQ zeigen", Nachschub hint, "Noch eins wünschen". Klassisch | Holotisch stays in the head too.

Three alternatives of HOW the head carries those four things (all three keep the d3 panel card for the actions):

- **d1 "Kennleiste"** — title left; on the right one grouped control strip on the title's baseline: `[Patch 4.10.2-LIVE ▾] [⌂ Im Hangar · Variante a ▾] [Klassisch | Holotisch]`. Not owned: the middle segment reads `◇ Nicht im Hangar` (quiet, click scrolls/highlights the panel card).
- **d2 "Unterzeile"** — under the title one sentence-like meta line with inline selects: `Aegis Dynamics · Abfangjäger · Patch 4.10.2-LIVE ▾ · ⌂ Im Hangar · Variante a ▾`; right of the title only Klassisch | Holotisch.
- **d3 "Plakette"** — a status badge right after the title (`IM HANGAR` filled / `NICHT IM HANGAR` outline); the variant as a subtitle select directly under the title (`Variante a ▾`, only when owned); patch select and Klassisch | Holotisch top right.

Screens per design (same four for each, click-dummy wired like round 1):
1. `dN-s1` "Schiff, nicht im Besitz" — Holotisch; panel card "Haben?" with Wunschliste + Hangar buttons.
2. `dN-s2` "Schiff im Hangar" — Holotisch; head shows Im Hangar + variant select; panel card "Dein Exemplar" (name „Schatten“, actions, Nachschub hint, Im HQ zeigen) WITHOUT the variant select (it moved to the head).
3. `dN-s3` "Klassisch, im Hangar" — same head, classic stage, the card on top of the side column.
4. `dN-s4` "Gegenstand im Inventar" — an item page (e.g. "CF-337 Panther Repeater", "Klaus & Werner · Repetierkanone S3"), head shows `Im Inventar · 2×` instead of Hangar/variant, no Klassisch | Holotisch switch (item pages have their own Silhouette | 3D-Modell switch in the stage, show it quietly in the stage), item-page layout: stage left, tabbed panel right (Werte / Herstellung / Kaufen & Info) with the ownership card on top.

Wiring: s1 card "Hangar" → s2; s2 Klassisch → s3; s3 Holotisch → s2. s4 standalone.

**Share corner (decided, same in all designs, not a variant):** draw the bottom-right corner in every screen: feedback launcher (56px disc) bottom-right, Teilen (44px) on the launcher's centre line directly left of it, β (44px) above the launcher. Remove the stage-toolbar "Teilen" (round 1 mock had none — keep it out). Show it as `position:absolute` inside `.vk` (no fixed). Keep the KPI strip clear of that lane (padding-right on `.vk-page` bottom or a right margin on the KPI strip). One small annotation on it per design is NOT wanted (it's decided) — instead mention it in the intro.
