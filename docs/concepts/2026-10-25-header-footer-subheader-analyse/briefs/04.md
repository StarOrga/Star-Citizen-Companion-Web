### So: 3 clearly different header ⇄ subheader DIRECTIONS (`data-design-set="alternatives"`)

All three share the confirmed rules (one bar system, subheader = always search, crumbs left, filters
fold out of the search, hides on content pages, stays on tool pages, fixed corners) but differ
radically in HOW header and subheader belong together and transform. Each must look genuinely
sexy/premium (Star Citizen HUD / MobiGlas inspired, but with today's background and centred
typography that the user likes). Ideas (refine, keep them far apart):
- **A · Fusion** — header and subheader are ONE capsule; scrolling collapses the brand + main nav
  into a compact segment inside the search capsule (header "Auswahl" migrates into the subheader).
- **B · Stack-Dock** — two thin layers stacked like a cockpit visor; on scroll the header slides up
  behind the subheader and its nav becomes a dropdown/tab strip at the left edge of the subheader.
- **C · Orbit** — the search is the centre; brand left and main nav right orbit/morph around it; on
  cinematic hubs the stage's search line flies up and becomes the bar (show this flight animated).
