## The big message of round 2's feedback

The user keeps saying: **don't redesign what exists — keep today's components and polish
them into the new look.** Verbatim on the Sternkarte: „wir haben doch das alles schon
designe.....d abrauchst du wenig nur ändern dran, nur polishen auf diesen style!“; on the
subheader: „Warum anderer subheader als auf main, bitte den von main beibehalten“; on
comet/guess tracks: „hierzu finde ich unsere akteulelle version besser“ / „hier auch das
aktuelle besser“. Round 3 must therefore be **closer to the real app**: today's shell
subheader, today's components, today's structure, only restyled (scene background, no
opaque boxes, shared frame spacing). Fewer invented elements. This should be the round
the user can say „Umsetzen“ to; keep annotations few and only for real open points.

## Facts: the real shell subheader (from #841, `src/app/shell/fusion-bar/`, `main-header/`)

Main pages (Verse, Codex, HQ) have a frameless cinematic header on the scene: brand logo,
fixed 112 px nav slots inside the page frame, the account menu top right. Past a scroll
threshold the page's staged search field flies up and docks into the „Satinkante“
subheader bar: logo slot (the header logo glides into it), a two-line plate (category +
back link to the origin, full path on hover), the search field and the Strg K hint.
Switching between main pages fades the old content out while the camera moves and lets
the new content rise in. Phones dock the bar at the thumb. → The round-2 mock invented
its own crumb/capsule subheader; replace it with this real subheader on every subpage
(plate shows e.g. „Verse“ / back link, path „Verse › Patches“). Back to the hub always
via the logo or the subheader's back link (user: „Immer über das logo bzw. den sub header
der ja schon da ist!“) — the reverse flight plays then; no extra back button.

## Decisions taken in round 2 (don't re-ask)

- Frame (view r2-v1): planet emblem belongs to the frame — include; aside/actions slot
  belongs to the frame — include, but note: „ja, wobei ich den noch nicht elegant finde,
  gern nochmal refinen!“ → show a more elegant aside/actions slot; fixed gap under the
  head — include.
- Kartograph badge (view r2-v2): „Chip im Kopf der Sternkarte“ included, but note: „chip
  finde ich lahm, warum hat nicht jede stufe ein wappen und man sieht das wappen
  anklickbar und man sieht wo man steht die nächsten drei und vergangenen drei“ → design
  a rank ladder: every rank has its own crest (Wappen); the current crest is clickable
  (to the badge page); show where you stand plus the previous three and next three ranks
  (locked ones dimmed). Lives on the Sternkarte (header aside or a band near the map).
  Settings / both / search-only: discarded.
- App teaser (view r2-v3): all four behaviours discarded — replaced by the new direction
  below.
